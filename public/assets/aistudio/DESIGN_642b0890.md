## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an institutional-grade, mission-critical aesthetic for an enterprise surveillance and integrated security technology firm. It projects unwavering operational readiness, precision engineering, and high-trust corporate authority.

The visual language bridges modern defense-grade command consoles with high-tier enterprise software architecture. It fuses deep nocturnal foundations with crisp, luminescent optical accents, delivering an interface that feels vigilant, technical, and impenetrable.

Key aesthetic characteristics:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Layered nocturnal depths dominated by deep space navy and carbon slate, punctuated by structural glass surfaces.
- **Controlled Luminescence:** Cyan and azure optics serve purely functional roles—directing visual triage, highlighting telemetry vectors, and confirming operational status.
- **Precision Metrics:** Monoline micro-borders, discrete data chips, tabular numerical formatting, and structural scanline discipline evoke advanced CCTV, telemetry diagnostics, and biometric access control grids.

## Colors

The palette balances surveillance command-center darkness with high-clarity contrast to ensure instant visual triage across multi-monitor dashboards, client portals, and administrative views.

### Foundation & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#060A17` (Deep Obsidian Void) — Primary application viewport background.
- **Surface Layer 1:** `#0B132B` (Command Navy) — Primary structural shell, sidebar navigation, and footer surfaces.
- **Surface Layer 2:** `#0F172A` (Slate Midnight) — Base card containers, data tables, and modal backgrounds.
- **Surface Elevated:** `#1E293B` (Interceptors Slate) — Hover states, nested cards, and input backgrounds.
- **Translucent Glass Surface:** `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.72)` — Elevated operational overlays and telemetry pods.

### Accents & Optics
- **Primary Optical (`#0EA5E9`):** Electric Azure. Used for dominant interactions, active surveillance streams, primary action buttons, and active tabs.
- **Primary Glow / Focus (`#38BDF8`):** Laser Cyan. Used for fine-line focus rings, active indicator pulses, and interactive hovering.
- **System Active (`#0284C7`):** Deep Blue Shift. Interactive press states and structured iconography.

### Status & Telemetry
- **Surveillance Nominal (Online / Armed):** `#10B981` (Emerald Guard)
- **Surveillance Anomaly (Alert / Motion):** `#F59E0B` (Amber Notice)
- **Surveillance Breach (Critical / Sensor Fault):** `#EF4444` (Infrared Critical)

### Typography & Content
- **Text High-Contrast:** `#F8FAFC` (Optical White) — Primary titles, live sensor readings, telemetry values.
- **Text Standard:** `#CBD5E1` (Muted Slate) — Body copy, field descriptors, subheaders.
- **Text Low-Contrast:** `#64748B` (Steel Neutral) — Watermarks, table headers, inactive metadata, timestamps.
- **Structural Borders:** `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.14)` and `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.12)` for crisp panel separation.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes extreme visual hierarchy, legibility at distance, and structured tabular presentation.

### Type Pairings
- **Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its commanding geometry, structured apexes, and clean corporate character. Renders titles, module headers, and enterprise claims with authoritative presence.
- **Body & Data (Inter):** Deployed for all content tiers, diagnostic feeds, form elements, and security tables. Provides robust x-height, neutral letterforms, and optimized kerning for numerical clarity.

### Implementation Guidelines
- Display and Hero elements must maintain tight negative letter-spacing to prevent visual leakage.
- Telemetry, sensor IDs, IP matrices, and camera status channels use `telemetry-code` with uppercase styling (`text-transform: uppercase`) and tabular figure formatting (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- Body content must not exceed 68 characters per line to guarantee operational scan speed during incident assessment.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored to a strict 8pt base grid with an inner 4pt sub-grid for high-density telemetry clusters, hardware spec sheets, and status arrays.

### Grid Philosophy
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid-grid architecture with a max container threshold of `1440px`. Column gutters remain locked to `gutter-lg` (`2rem`) for wide visual pacing, with canvas margins at `margin-desktop` (`3rem`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout. Gutters compress to `gutter` (`1.5rem`), outer margins scale to `margin` (`2rem`). Video feed matrices reflow from 4-up to 2-up grids.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Margin snaps to `margin-mobile` (`1rem`) and gutters tighten to `gutter-sm` (`1rem`). Split navigation collapses to an anchored command bar; camera and access feeds transition to vertical stack cards.

### Density Tiers
- **Comfortable (Public Pages / Solution Architecture):** Utilizes `space-lg` and `space-xl` for spacious corporate storytelling and high-contrast section isolation.
- **Compact (Telemetry Monitoring / CCTV Matrices):** Employs `space-xs` and `space-sm` for high-density dashboard layouts, minimizing dead space between live feeds and hardware diagnostics.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses architectural backdrops, translucent glass layers, and hairline luminosity borders rather than heavy standard drop shadows. This creates the ambiance of an advanced surveillance operating center.

### Depth Layers
- **Ground Floor (Base Matrix):** Solid background (`#060A17`) paired with a fine vector grid pattern (`rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.03)` at 32px increments) to evoke geospatial blueprints.
- **Layer 1 (Structural Shells):** Solid `#0B132B` panels bound by `1px` borders in `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.1)`. No drop shadow.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Cards & Feeds):** Polished glassmorphism with `background: rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)`, backdrop blur of `16px`, and a perimeter hairline stroke of `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.18)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals, Overlays & Camera Focus):** `background: rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.92)`, backdrop blur of `24px`, bound by a crisp border `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)`. Drops an ambient cyan-tinted shadow: `0 20px 48px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 24px 0 rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.12)`.

### Optical Highlights & Light Paths
- Elevation gains prominence through directional edge lighting: top borders of cards receive a subtle linear gradient border transition from `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.35)` to `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.02)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework uses intentional soft angles (`roundedness: 1`). Surfaces avoid soft organic curves in favor of industrial, calibrated form factors that communicate physical durability and hardware precision.

### Geometric Rules
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** `4px` (`0.25rem`) border radius. This subtle radius softens digital glare while maintaining sharp edges.
- **Module Cards & Video Canvas:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) border radius. Frame containers remain clean and architectural.
- **System Overlays & Floating Panels:** `12px` (`0.75rem`) border radius.
- **Telemetry Indicators & Badges:** Fixed `2px` micro-radius or circular indicators (`50%`) strictly when communicating active hardware status or recording beacons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Command:** Solid `#0EA5E9` with `#060A17` text, font weight 600. `4px` radius. Hover shifts to `#38BDF8` with a subtle glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.4)`.
- **Secondary Surveillance:** `#0F172A` background, `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)` border, `#F8FAFC` text. Hover transitions border to `#38BDF8` with background `#1E293B`.
- **Ghost / Neutral:** Transparent background, `1px solid transparent`, `#CBD5E1` text. Hover adds `background: rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.08)` and crisp border `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)`.

### Cards & Video Feeds
- Built on Layer 2 Glassmorphism: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.72)` background, `16px` backdrop filter blur, and a `1px` border of `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.16)`.
- Cards feature top-edge micro-highlights and optional corner tick marks (2px x 2px visual alignment markers) to mimic physical monitor displays.
- Live stream card headers embed technical metadata: Camera ID, Resolution (4K/1080P), Bitrate, and real-time FPS.

### Telemetry Badges & Chips
- Compact, uppercase typography (`telemetry-code`).
- **Live / Recording:** Dark emerald background (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`), `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`, text `#10B981`. Includes a pulsing `6px` beacon dot.
- **Network / Protocol:** `rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.1)` background, `1px solid rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.3)`, text `#38BDF8`.
- **Tamper Alert:** `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.15)` background, `1px solid rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.4)`, text `#EF4444`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Inputs:** Filled `#0B132B` background with `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)`. Text in `#F8FAFC`. Placeholder in `#64748B`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts instantly to `#0EA5E9` with an outer outline ring of `2px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.25)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Precision square and circular markers with dark fill `#0B132B`, bordered in `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)`. Checked state introduces an electric cyan fill (`#0EA5E9`) with an obsidian micro-glyph.

### Data Tables & Event Logs
- Alternating rows using `#0B132B` and `#0F172A`.
- Header row pinned with uppercase `11px` typography, `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.15)` bottom divider, and neutral-colored labels.
- Timestamp column rendered in fixed tabular digits with monospaced precision.

### Specialized Hardware & Access Widgets
- **Biometric / Access State Module:** Dual-state cards visualizing gate locks, NFC card readers, and turnstile activity with high-contrast indicator tracks.
- **PTZ Directional Control Pod:** Polished slate ring with directional vectors illuminated in azure upon hover, providing tactical visual feedback.
